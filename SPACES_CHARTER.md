# A11-K Spaces

Purpose: provide distinct workspaces for thinking, building, review, and recovery without mixing contexts.

## Spaces
- Focus: active work only.
- Forge: experiments and prototypes.
- Review: decisions, evidence, and release gates.
- Archive: preserved history.
- Private: owner-only material.

Every workspace declares its sensitivity, retention, allowed agents, and export policy. Cross-space movement creates an explicit transfer record.
